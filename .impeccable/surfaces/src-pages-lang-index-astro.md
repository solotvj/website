# Studio homepage

Scope: `src/pages/[...lang]/index.astro`, Spanish and English. Mode: Persuade.

- Audience and job: owners of small businesses in Argentina, mostly on a phone, who should recognise their problem, choose a product and write on WhatsApp. Custom work is a third, smaller door.
- Proof: the products themselves and how Solotvj works. No customers, logos, testimonials or figures exist; none may be invented.
- Untouched: the assistant's former homepage, moved whole to `/asistente`; `/precios`; legal pages.
- Chosen over three rounds (2026-11-16): "How we work, first", on bone, ending on an index card at the side of the commitments. A strip of product tiles read as a second navigation bar, and three cards with proof were built and rejected by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio is introduced by its work and its word: three things you can pick up, then three promises it keeps. It refuses the agency homepage that opens on a slogan and a grid of services.

OWN-WORLD: Solotvj's Expressive mode as built in `loca.css`: bone ground, ink 2px borders, hard offset ink shadows, square corners, white paper cards tilted by up to 2 degrees, one blue for the action and the italic phrase, yellow for the one card that is not a product yet. Barlow Condensed at display scale, Barlow for everything functional, JetBrains Mono only for status words.

STORY: The visitor sees what Solotvj makes, each shown doing its job; reads that Solotvj builds, runs and answers for it; opens their product or writes on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the wordmark, the price link and the contact button. Left, the three commitments stacked as condensed lines at up to 114px on desktop, so each stays on one line beside the card, (Lo hacemos. Lo operamos. Y respondemos, the last in italic blue), each with one sentence under it. Right, pinned at the top and tilted 1.5 degrees, a white index card headed "Qué hacemos" with three numbered rows: the WhatsApp assistant, the rental system and, on yellow, custom software. Each row is a link with a one-line description and a drawn arrow. On a phone the card comes first, then the lines.

FORM: Index card beside stacked commitments; the user's final pick ("Index card at the side") after rejecting the built "Three cards with proof" (2026-11-16), from the dealt structure "How we work, first" (position 6 of 7). seed:dd6bfc8496aa72cc. Signature interaction: a row of the index floods blue and its arrow advances on hover and focus; nothing else moves.
