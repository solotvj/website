# Card

A bounded block of related content: a service, a case study, a plan, a settings group.

**Provide:** `children` (body copy or content), optionally `eyebrow`, `title`, `footer` (meta or actions), `tone`, and `as` for the element (`section` by default, `article` for case studies).

- `default` (`surface-100` with a hairline) for most content.
- `inset` (`surface-200`) for secondary groups inside a page section.
- `bone` for the one card per page that should stand out: a quote, a pricing plan, a highlighted case. Never more than one.
- Keep cards flat: hairlines, no shadows.
