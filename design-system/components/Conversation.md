# Conversation

A framed WhatsApp transcript with the contact's name and status, holding `ChatBubble`s.

**Provide:** `title` (the contact or business name), `subtitle` (number or channel), `status` (usually a `Badge`), `children` (`ChatBubble`s in order).

- Use it in the product's inbox view and as the main illustration on the website instead of stock photos.
- Anonymise real transcripts before showing them publicly.
