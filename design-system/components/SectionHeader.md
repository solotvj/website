# SectionHeader

The opening of a page section: a mono eyebrow, a serif title, an optional lede and actions.

**Provide:** `title`, and optionally `eyebrow` (two to four words, set uppercase automatically), `lede` (one or two sentences), `actions` (buttons), `size="xl"` for the hero only, `align="center"`, `level` for the heading tag.

- One `xl` header per page, at the top.
- Titles are statements, in sentence case, without a final full stop unless they are a full sentence.
- Keep the lede under about 30 words; put detail in the section itself.
