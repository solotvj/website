# Data processing annex

**Last updated:** 13 October 2026\
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Parties and purpose

This annex is part of the [Terms of service](/terms) and is accepted together with them. It
governs the processing of the personal data of the people who write to the Customer on
WhatsApp (its customers or patients, "Data Subjects"), which Solotvj carries out on the
Customer's behalf.

- **The Customer** is the **controller** of that data and of the database it forms.
- **Solotvj** acts as **processor**, under article 25 of Law No. 25,326.

Capitalised words have the meaning given in the Terms and in the
[Privacy policy](/privacy).

---

## 2. Instructions and purpose

Solotvj processes the data **only on the Customer's instructions**, which are those in the
Terms, this annex and the configuration the Customer sets in the console, and **only to
provide the service**: receiving messages, replying with the business's information,
passing to the Customer's team the conversations that need it, transcribing voice notes and
showing conversations in the console.

Solotvj **does not use the data for its own purposes**, does not transfer it to third
parties — not even for storage — beyond the providers in section 5, and does not use it for
advertising or to train models. If an instruction from the Customer seemed unlawful to
Solotvj, it would tell the Customer before following it.

---

## 3. Data and Data Subjects

- **Data Subjects:** the people who write to the Customer's WhatsApp Business number.
- **Data:** the identifier and, where available, the phone number and WhatsApp profile
  name; the content of messages and voice notes; data the Data Subject provides in the
  conversation; and technical delivery data.
- **Sensitive data:** if the Customer's business is healthcare, messages may include
  health data. The Customer declares that it processes them on the legal basis that applies
  to its activity — for example, that of article 8 of Law No. 25,326 for healthcare
  establishments and professionals, under professional secrecy — and Solotvj processes them
  under the same duty of secrecy.

---

## 4. Confidentiality and security

- Solotvj and everyone acting on its behalf keep the data **secret**, also after the
  service ends (article 10 of Law No. 25,326).
- Solotvj applies the **security measures** described in section 13 of the
  [Privacy policy](/privacy#13-information-security), including encryption in transit and at
  rest, isolation between Customers and least-privilege access.
- **Incidents:** if Solotvj becomes aware of an incident affecting the Customer's data, it
  will notify the Customer without undue delay, with the information available, so the
  Customer can meet its own obligations.

---

## 5. Providers (sub-processors) and international transfers

- The Customer **authorises** Solotvj to use the providers listed in section 8.1 of the
  [Privacy policy](/privacy), for the purposes stated there.
- Solotvj imposes on them confidentiality, purpose-limitation and security obligations
  equivalent to this annex's, and is answerable to the Customer for their compliance.
- The Customer **authorises the international transfers** to those providers described in
  section 9 of the Policy, under the conditions stated there.
- If Solotvj adds or replaces a provider that receives Data Subjects' data, it will tell the
  Customer at least **30 days** in advance, as the Terms provide. If the Customer disagrees,
  it can cancel before the change applies.

---

## 6. Data Subjects' rights

Solotvj **assists the Customer** in answering Data Subjects' requests for access,
rectification, updating and deletion:

- if a request reaches Solotvj, it forwards it to the Customer without delay;
- the console lets the Customer delete a contact's data at the Data Subject's request; and
- Solotvj gives the Customer the technical information it needs to answer.

Requests concerning minors must come from their legal representative.

---

## 7. The Customer's obligations

The Customer agrees to:

- tell Data Subjects, before or at the start of the conversation, that an automated
  assistant serves them, who controls their data and how to exercise their rights, and
  obtain consent where required;
- register its database with the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública when the law
  requires it. Solotvj provides the technical information about the service it needs to do
  so;
- not ask, through the assistant, for data that isn't needed for its activity.

---

## 8. Retention, return and destruction

- During the service, data is kept for the periods in section 10 of the
  [Privacy policy](/privacy).
- When the service ends, Solotvj **returns** the data to the Customer through the export
  and then **destroys** it, backups included, within the periods in section 10 of the
  [Terms](/terms).

---

## 9. Information and audit

On request, Solotvj will make available to the Customer the reasonable information needed
to show compliance with this annex.

---

## 10. Term

This annex applies while Solotvj processes data on the Customer's behalf, including after
the service ends, until the data is destroyed. Anything not covered here is governed by the
Terms and the Privacy policy.
