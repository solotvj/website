## 2. What the service does

Solotvj provides dental practices, clinics and small businesses (the "Customer") with an
automated assistant that answers WhatsApp messages on the Customer's behalf. It answers
common questions from information the Customer supplies, takes appointment requests, and
hands the conversation to the Customer's staff when appropriate.

It runs on the **Meta WhatsApp Business Platform** and uses third-party language models to
compose replies (section 8).

**The assistant always identifies itself as a virtual assistant.** It does not present
itself as a human.

**The assistant only replies.** It does not start conversations, send reminders,
confirmations or promotions on its own initiative. Every exchange begins with a message
from you. Were we to add such messages in future, they would require your prior opt-in and
this policy would be updated first.

## 3. Our role: two distinct situations

### 3.1 When you message a practice or business

- **The Customer is the data controller.** They decide what data is requested, why, and
  for how long it is kept.
- **Solotvj is the data processor.** We process that data solely on the Customer's
  instructions.

That relationship is documented: **we enter into a data processing agreement with every
Customer**, setting out instructions, authorised purposes, security measures,
confidentiality and the fate of the data on termination (section 10.3), as required by
Article 25 of Law No. 25.326.

**To access, correct or delete your data, your first point of contact is the practice or
business you messaged.** You may also contact us directly (sections 11 and 12).

### 3.2 When you are our Customer, or a website visitor

Here **Solotvj is the controller** for your contact, billing and account data.

## 4. What data we process

### 4.1 Data of people who message a Customer via WhatsApp

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp phone number, or the Meta-assigned user identifier under coexistence mode | Meta |
| WhatsApp profile name | Meta |
| Content of the messages you send (text) | You |
| Voice notes and their transcription | You |
| Message metadata: timestamp, message ID, delivery and read status | Meta |
| Information you volunteer: name, reason for consultation, availability, insurance | You |
| Your conversation history with that Customer | Generated by the service |
| A record that the privacy notice was sent to your number, and when (section 6.1) | Generated by the service |

### 4.2 Sensitive data

**The reason for a dental or other health-related consultation may constitute health
data**, which is **sensitive data** under Article 2 of Law No. 25.326.

We do not ask for clinical information. The assistant is configured **not** to request it,
**not** to diagnose, **not** to assess symptom severity and **not** to recommend
medication. If you describe a symptom on your own initiative, that text remains in the
conversation history and receives the heightened treatment described in **sections 10
(retention) and 13 (security)**.

We recommend you **do not send detailed clinical information, test results, medical images
or third parties' data over WhatsApp.**

### 4.3 Customer data

Name and legal name, tax ID, address, contact email and phone, billing details, admin panel
credentials, and the business information uploaded to feed the assistant.

### 4.4 Technical and website data

Server and application logs: IP addresses, request timestamps, response codes, errors.

Our website **uses no analytics or advertising cookies and no tracking tools**. However,
being hosted by a third party, your visit is logged by that provider. Website providers are
listed in section 8.2.

## 5. What we use the data for

1. **Answer your question** using information the Customer provided.
2. **Take your appointment request** and pass it to the Customer's staff.
3. **Hand the conversation to a person** when you ask, or when warranted.
4. **Notify the Customer's staff** that a conversation needs attention.
5. **Operate and maintain the service.**
6. **Evidence our compliance** with the information and consent duties (hence the record
   of the notice in section 6.1).
7. **Comply with legal obligations.**

### What we expressly do not do

- No selling, renting or transferring your personal data.
- No advertising, ours or anyone else's.
- No commercial profiling.
- **No training of artificial intelligence models**, by us or by our providers. This is
  established contractually with the providers in section 8.
- No using one Customer's data to serve another. Customers are isolated at database level.
- No unsolicited messages (section 2).

## 6. Consent and prior information

### 6.1 How consent is given

Article 5 of Law No. 25.326 requires consent to be **free, express and informed**, given
**in writing or by equivalent means**. The mechanism is:

1. **The first time you message** a Customer through this channel, the assistant replies
   with a notice that identifies itself as an automated assistant, states that the
   conversation is processed by Solotvj as the Customer's technology provider, **links to
   this policy**, and explains that continuing the conversation means accepting this
   processing.
2. That notice is sent **once per contact number**, and the date and time of sending are
   recorded solely to evidence that the information was given.
3. If you do not wish to continue, **simply do not reply**. You may also write `BAJA`
   (unsubscribe) at any time and we will stop processing your messages on this channel
   (section 12).

Your subsequent reply, sent in writing from your own phone number, constitutes the
statement of will the law requires.

### 6.2 Legal basis

- **Your consent**, given as in 6.1 (Article 5).
- **The professional or contractual relationship** with the Customer, where processing is
  necessary to perform it (Article 5(2)(d)).
- **Article 8 of Law No. 25.326**, which permits healthcare establishments and health
  professionals to collect and process health data of their own patients, subject to
  professional secrecy. Solotvj processes such data only as the Customer's processor and
  under the same duty of confidence.

You may **withdraw consent** at any time, prospectively (section 12).

### 6.3 Which data is required, and what happens if you withhold it

Per Article 6 of Law No. 25.326:

| Data | Required? | If you withhold it |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number, profile name, message content | **Unavoidable** — the WhatsApp channel carries them by design | This channel cannot be used without them. Phone or in person remain available |
| Name, reason for consultation, availability, for an appointment request | **Voluntary** | We cannot register the request through this channel. The Customer will still assist you by its usual means |
| Insurance | **Voluntary** | We can only give general private-rate pricing |
| Any symptom or clinical detail | **Never required.** The assistant neither needs nor requests it | None: the appointment request proceeds regardless |

**Consequences of inaccurate data:** if your name, contact number or availability are
wrong, the Customer's staff may be unable to confirm the appointment or reach you, and the
appointment may be misallocated. You can correct these at any time (section 11).

## 9. International data transfers

Several providers in section 8 are established outside Argentina. In particular, message
content is sent to **Meta, OpenRouter, OpenAI and Anthropic** in the **United States**,
which is **not** on the list of countries recognised by the Argentine
authority as providing an adequate level of protection. These are therefore
**international transfers** under Article 12 of Law No. 25.326.

They rely on:

- **model contractual clauses** entered into with each provider, under **Disposición
  No. 60-E/2016** of the former National Directorate for Personal Data Protection (model
  for the provision of data processing services), and/or the Ibero-American Data
  Protection Network clauses approved by **AAIP Resolución No. 198/2023**, which
  supplements it; and
- **your informed consent**, given with knowledge of this section.

Those agreements impose confidentiality, purpose limitation, security measures and a
prohibition on use for model training. You may request the status of each at the address
in section 16.

**The Customer and billing database**, for which Solotvj is the controller and which is
declared under Legajo RL-2026-95041698-APN-DNPDP#AAIP (section 7), **is also subject to
international transfer**:

- to **Brazil**, where Hostinger hosts the application and database; and
- to the **United States**, where Cloudflare R2 stores the encrypted backups and Google
  Workspace handles email with each Customer's contact persons.

Neither country is on the list of countries with an adequate level of protection. These
transfers rely on the same model contractual clauses described above.

## 10. How long we keep data

### 10.1 Retention periods

Set by the Customer as controller. Unless configured otherwise, our defaults are:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Message content and conversation history | 12 months from the last message |
| Voice note audio files | Deleted **immediately after transcription**; never more than 7 days |
| Audio transcriptions | Same as message content |
| Record of the privacy notice (date and time) | Duration of the Customer relationship plus 5 years, as evidence of the information duty |
| Technical and access logs | 90 days |
| Encrypted backups | 30 days rolling |
| Customer billing and administration data | As required by tax and commercial law |

On expiry, data is removed by automated purge processes.

### 10.2 Retention at the language model provider

**Current position:** the primary provider retains requests for **up to 30 days** solely to
detect abuse, and does not use them to train models. **We do not currently have a
zero-data-retention arrangement in place.** If we obtain one, this section and the policy
date will be updated.

### 10.3 What happens when a Customer leaves

Under Article 25(2) of Law No. 25.326, on termination of a Customer relationship we:

- offer **export** of the data processed on their behalf, in a readable format, within
  **30 days**; and
- **permanently delete** all such data from our systems and backups within **30 days**
  of that export or, if no export is requested, of termination.

We keep no data belonging to departed Customers, absent a legal obligation to the contrary.

### 10.4 Outside our control

Message content also remains in the WhatsApp application on your own phone and on the
Customer's, per each party's settings. That is not ours to delete.

## 11. Your rights

Under Articles 14, 15, 16 and 17 of Law No. 25.326 you have the right of **access**,
**rectification and updating**, **deletion** (section 12), **confidentiality**, and to
**withdraw consent** prospectively.

### How to exercise them

As explained in section 3, **if you messaged a practice or business, contact that Customer
first**, as controller. Otherwise write to **soporte@solotvj.com.ar** and we will
coordinate with them.

To identify you we will ask for the WhatsApp number you messaged from and the name of the
Customer. If your identity must be verified with your national ID (DNI), **showing it is
enough**. Only where an image is indispensable will we ask for **the front of the DNI
only**, with the procedure number (número de trámite), the barcode and any other
unnecessary data obscured. If you act on behalf of someone else, you must provide proof
of that representation.

### Deadlines

- **Access:** answered within **ten (10) calendar days** of identity being established
  (Article 14(2)).
- **Rectification, updating or deletion:** resolved within **five (5) business days** of
  receipt (Article 16(2)).

### Statutory notices

> The data subject has the right to exercise the right of access to their data free of
> charge at intervals of not less than six months, unless a legitimate interest to the
> contrary is demonstrated, as established in Article 14(3) of Law No. 25.326.

> The **Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP)**, as supervisory authority under
> Law No. 25.326, has the power to hear complaints and claims relating to non-compliance
> with personal data protection rules.

## 12. How to delete your data

You can request deletion by any of these routes:

**1. On WhatsApp, in the same conversation.**
Send **`BAJA`**. The assistant will stop processing your messages and your request will be
passed to the Customer for erasure.

**2. By email.**
Write to **soporte@solotvj.com.ar** stating the WhatsApp number you messaged from
and the name of the practice or business you contacted.

**3. Directly to the practice or business**, which is the controller.

We verify identity by the same standard as in section 11: showing your DNI is enough;
where an image is indispensable, only the front, with the procedure number and barcode
obscured; and anyone acting on another person's behalf must prove that representation.

### What we delete

The conversation history, the data you provided (name, reason, availability), voice note
transcriptions, and the link between your number and that conversation.

### What we cannot delete

- Messages stored on **your own phone** and on the Customer's.
- Data already in the Customer's **medical record**, governed by health legislation and its
  own retention periods.
- Copies in **encrypted backups**, overwritten in the normal rotation cycle (30 days).
- Records we must retain by **legal obligation**.

### Timeframe

Deletion is carried out within **five (5) business days** of the request and verification
of your identity (Article 16(2)), and confirmed to you by the same means you used.

## 13. Information security

- **Encryption in transit** (TLS) for all communications.
- **Encryption at rest** for the database and backups.
- **Isolation between Customers** at database level.
- **Encrypted third-party platform credentials**, with separately managed keys and periodic
  rotation.
- **Cryptographic authenticity verification** of every inbound message, rejecting requests
  not originating from Meta.
- **Logs without unnecessary personal data**: message content and full phone numbers are
  kept out of technical logs.
- **Least-privilege access.**

No security measure is absolute. Should an incident affect your data, we will notify the
responsible Customer without undue delay so they can meet their notification obligations.

## 14. Automated processing

The assistant is automated and uses language models to compose replies.

**It takes no decision producing legal or medical effects concerning you.** It does not
diagnose, assess symptom severity, recommend medication, confirm appointments on its own,
or turn patients away. Every clinical or scheduling decision is taken by a person on the
Customer's staff.

It can only answer using information the Customer previously uploaded, and is designed and
tested to recognise when it does not hold a fact and route the question to a person rather
than improvise.

You can ask to speak to a person at any time.
