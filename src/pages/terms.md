## 2. What the service includes

- **Setup:** we load your business's information, connect your WhatsApp Business number
  and test the assistant before turning it on.

  hour, using only your business's information, and passes to your team the conversations

## 14. What the assistant does and doesn't do

- It replies **only with the information you gave us**. When it doesn't know something, it
  doesn't make it up: it passes the conversation to your team.
- Even so, like any automated system, **it can make mistakes**. Review your business's
  information in the console and tell us if a reply was wrong.
- **It does not replace your team's professional judgement.** It is not meant for
  emergencies, diagnoses, or medical, legal or financial advice.
- If yours is a healthcare business, you define the protocol for urgent cases — for
  example, the on-call phone number the assistant gives — and clinical decisions are always
  your team's.
- It does not send messages on its own initiative: it only replies to whoever writes.
