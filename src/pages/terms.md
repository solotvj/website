## 2. What the service includes

- **Setup:** we load your business's information, connect your WhatsApp Business number
  and test the assistant before turning it on.
- **The assistant:** it replies to your customers on your WhatsApp Business number, at any
  hour, using only your business's information, and passes to your team the conversations
  that need a person.
- **The console:** a dashboard where you and your team see conversations, reply and update

- **Maintenance:** we keep the service running and test every information change before it
  reaches your customers.

## 6. Late payment

If an invoice is not paid when due:

1. We send you a reminder.
2. **10 days** after the due date, we pause the assistant: it stops replying until payment

   business's information is kept.
3. **30 days** after the due date, we cancel the service, with the effects in section 8.

While the assistant is paused, it is turned back on when you pay what is owed, with no new
setup fee.

- It replies **only with the information you gave us**. When it doesn't know something, it

- **It does not replace your team's professional judgement.** It is not meant for

- It does not send messages on its own initiative: it only replies to whoever writes.
